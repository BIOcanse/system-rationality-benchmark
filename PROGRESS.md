# Progress

## Goal

Public results of the System Rationality benchmark (系统合理性测试): score and cost by model and reasoning effort, in English and Chinese. The test itself (scoring items, reports, tested source) stays private in the author's local project.

## Done

- 2026-10-22: Repository renamed from `llm-efficiency-cost-charts`; its earlier content, releases and history were deleted (user: "早期内容完全删了即可").
- 2026-10-22: Round 3 published: 28 runs, 42 scoring items, charts in English and Chinese (PNG, SVG), results CSV.

## How to update

The files under `charts/` and `data/` are generated by `rationality/resource-manager/run/export_public.py` in the private project (`python export_public.py --repo <this repository> [round]`). Re-run it after the scoring record or prices change, then update the dates and counts in both READMEs and this file.
